1. What problem are we solving?

Cloud engineers spend significant time analyzing resource utilization, identifying waste, forecasting costs, and recommending optimizations. Existing tools often require switching between multiple dashboards and lack contextual AI explanations.

2. Our Solution

Cloud Resource Optimization Copilot is an AI-assisted platform that consolidates cloud metrics, predicts future resource utilization and costs, recommends optimization opportunities, and explains insights in natural language.
